感谢您使用 TideTone（"潮音"）。本隐私政策说明了我们如何收集、使用和保护您的个人信息。

1. 我们收集的信息

我们收集以下用于提供应用功能的信息。可选使用统计另在下方说明。

  • Apple ID（匿名）：通过 Sign in with Apple 登录时，Apple 提供一个匿名的用户标识符，用于识别您的账户。我们不会获取您真实的 Apple ID 或电子邮件地址（除非您主动授权）。
  • 用户创建内容：您在 App 中创建的音乐谱子数据，用于云端同步和分享功能。
  • 应用偏好设置：主题、语言、音频设置等，存储在您的设备本地。

可选的使用统计

在提供使用统计的 TideTone 版本中，分享默认关闭。您可在「设置 → 分享使用统计」明确同意后开启。统计从同意后开始，涵盖首次打开 App、首次成功播放、保存或导入、使用 Pro 功能的入口、付费页展示、所选套餐、购买尝试及结果、恢复购买结果，以及升级后是否完成原操作。我们还会记录 Apple 验证通过的 Pro 交易是在购买、恢复购买还是后台更新时确认的。

PDF 使用统计包括预览原因和结果（首次预览、调整排版或重试）、打开升级页的原因（查看后续页或导出）、导出尝试及结果，以及操作时的免费或 Pro 状态。视频使用统计包括预览渲染的原因和结果（首次预览、调整设置、访问权限变化或重试）、免费或 Pro 状态、播放开始和播完一轮、从完整预览或导出入口打开升级页，以及生成和保存视频各自的尝试和结果。

单谱和曲谱包分享统计包括相应预览（接收者视角预览或曲谱包预览）的展示及当时的免费或 Pro 状态、点击升级、生成分享码的尝试和结果（成功、取消或失败），以及 App 将分享码写入剪贴板的操作。升级后生成有效分享码即计为完成原操作。这些记录无法确认分享码是否已发送或接收，也无法确认接收者是否已导入曲谱或曲谱包。

在官方曲库的曲目预览中，我们会记录预览展示及当时的免费或 Pro 状态、放大曲谱、每次点击试听、试听音频实际开始播放及结果（播完、取消或失败），以及点击升级、点击开始完整练习、进入练习页和完整曲谱实际开始播放。每次预览使用同一个随机编号来关联该流程中的购买或恢复购买，每次试听另有一个随机编号，用于关联本次请求、实际播放开始和结果。购买或恢复购买成功后，只有在同一流程中点击开始完整练习、进入练习页并实际开始播放完整曲谱，才计为完成原操作。这部分统计也不包含手碟型号。

在云同步状态页，我们会记录页面展示及当时的免费或 Pro 状态、点击升级，以及购买或恢复购买成功后返回该页面。对于与当次状态页访问相关的 Pro 同步，我们会记录是自动还是手动发起的，以及结果（成功、部分成功、冲突、待完成、失败或取消）。临时随机编号用于关联同一次页面流程，并配对同一次同步的尝试和结果。统计不包含账户、设备、安装或 iCloud 标识，也不包含曲谱标识、名称、内容、数量或原始错误详情。

识谱统计包括选择的材料类型（图片或 PDF）、显示的额度状态、当时的使用权限（免费或 Pro）、点击升级、购买或恢复购买成功后返回识谱流程，以及主动点击继续。识别统计包括实际开始、启动方式（选择材料或主动继续）、结果（成功、失败或取消）及识别成果实际展示。对于原生 TideTone PDF，我们会单独记录预览实际展示及你明确确认且还原实际完成。临时随机编号仅用于关联同一流程或尝试中的步骤。识谱统计不用于追踪用户，也不上传原始图片或 PDF、识别文字、文件名、曲谱数据、手机号码、账户标识、音阶、拍号或精确的额度次数。 识谱统计也适用本节的保留规则，原始记录、备份和汇总报表目前没有固定的自动删除期限。

灵感生曲统计包括设置页实际展示及当时的免费或 Pro 使用权限、显示的额度状态、点击升级、购买或恢复购买成功后返回设置页，以及主动点击继续。我们也会记录生成实际开始、启动方式（生成或继续）、结果（成功、失败或取消）和成果实际展示。临时随机编号仅用于关联同一流程或尝试的步骤。这些统计不上传动机音符、律动、风格、曲式长度、速度、曲谱名称、标识或内容、精确额度次数、原始错误详情、音频、文件、账户或设备标识及交易标识。如果你此前已同意分享使用统计，范围更新后需要重新明确同意。我们不会补记同意前的操作。

高级读谱统计包括连续滚动或分页阅读预览的实际展示、所选模式及当时的免费或 Pro 状态。我们也会记录内置固定演示实际开始播放及结果（播完或取消）、点击打开升级页，以及购买或恢复购买成功后返回阅读方式选择。我们会记录您通过选择模式或返回后点击应用按钮而主动应用模式的操作，以及原谱实际以所选模式显示。在同一预览流程中，点击升级、购买或恢复购买成功并返回后，您还需主动应用同一模式，且原谱实际以该模式显示，才计为完成原操作。临时随机编号仅用于关联同一流程或尝试中的步骤。这些统计不上传您的原谱、曲谱标题或标识、音符、音阶、手碟型号、速度、曲谱中的精确位置、文件、音频、视频或账户、设备、安装及交易标识。

转调统计包括预览实际展示及免费或 Pro 状态、原谱或转调后试听的请求、实际播放开始和结果（播完、失败或取消）、点击升级、购买或恢复购买成功后返回预览，以及主动保存副本或保存并打开的尝试和结果（成功、仅草稿保存成功、失败或取消）。同一流程中升级并返回后，正式保存副本成功才计为完成原操作。临时随机编号仅关联同一流程或尝试；不上传谱名、曲谱标识或内容、音符、音阶、手碟型号、转调映射、位置、文件、音频、账户、设备或交易标识。

记录还包含事件发生时间、服务器接收时间、App 版本和运行环境。随机生成的编号用于区分单条记录，或关联同一流程或同一次导出、生成或保存尝试。单谱和曲谱包分享可通过随机流程编号关联该流程中的分享操作、购买或恢复购买。

这些记录用于了解和改进 TideTone 的功能及购买体验。我们不会将这些记录用于广告追踪或出售给他人。TideTone App 上传的统计不包含手机号码、账户、设备或安装标识、原始交易号、收据、付款详情、曲谱包名称、曲谱名称和内容、所选曲谱的标识、分享码、剪贴板内容、PDF 或视频文件及其内容、音频或照片。

记录通过 HTTPS 发送至 TideTone 使用的腾讯云服务器。原始记录保存在该服务器上，备份存放在腾讯云的私有对象存储（COS）中。服务器上的原始记录及备份目前没有固定的自动删除期限，可能无限期保留。

您可随时关闭「分享使用统计」。设备会停止收集和后续上传，并清除尚未发送的记录。关闭分享不影响购买、恢复购买或 Pro 权益。未发送的记录超过 90 天后不再上传。服务器已接收的记录和备份不会因关闭分享或删除账户而自动删除。统计不带账户标识，因此我们无法凭账户自动查找或删除该账户对应的统计记录。如有隐私或删除请求，请发邮件至 Tidetoneapp@gmail.com。

Optional usage analytics

In versions of TideTone that offer usage analytics, sharing is off by default. To opt in, go to Settings → Share Usage Analytics and confirm your choice. After you opt in, TideTone records the first app open, the first successful play, save, or import, how you enter Pro features, paywall views, selected plans, purchase attempts and outcomes, restore outcomes, whether an Apple-verified Pro transaction was observed during a purchase, a restore, or a background update, and whether you completed the original action after upgrading.

PDF usage records include the reason for a preview update (initial preview, layout change, or retry) and its outcome, why you opened the upgrade screen (to view more pages or export), export attempts and outcomes, and whether you had free or Pro access during the relevant action. Video usage records include the reason for rendering the preview (first preview, settings change, access change, or retry) and its outcome, free or Pro access, the start and completion of one playback cycle, whether the upgrade screen was opened from the full preview or export, and attempts and outcomes for generating and saving the video.

Sharing records for single scores and multi-score packs include views of the relevant preview (Recipient Preview or Pack Preview) and whether access was free or Pro, taps to upgrade, attempts to generate a share code and their outcomes (success, cancellation, or failure), and when the app writes the code to the clipboard. Generating a valid share code after upgrading counts as completing the original action. These records cannot confirm whether the code was sent or received, or whether the recipient imported a score or pack.

For piece previews in the official library, we record when the preview appears and whether access is free or Pro, whether you enlarge the score, each request to listen to the excerpt, when its audio actually starts, and whether it finishes, is cancelled, or fails. We also record taps to upgrade, taps to start full practice, when the practice screen opens, and when the full score actually starts playing. A random ID links each preview flow to purchases or restores in that flow. Each listen request has its own random ID linking the request, playback start, and outcome. After a successful purchase or restore, the original action counts as complete only when you tap to start full practice, open the practice screen, and start full-score playback in the same flow. These records also omit the handpan model.

For Cloud Sync, we record when the status screen is shown and whether access is free or Pro, taps to upgrade, and returns to the screen after a successful purchase or restore. For Pro sync attempts associated with that screen session, we record whether the attempt was automatic or manual and its outcome (success, partial success, conflict, pending, failure, or cancellation). Temporary random IDs link steps in the same flow and match each attempt with its outcome. These records omit account, device, installation, and iCloud identifiers, as well as score identifiers, names, contents, counts, and raw error details.

For score recognition, we record the type of material you select (image or PDF), the quota status shown, whether access was free or Pro, taps to upgrade, returns to the recognition flow after a successful purchase or restore, and taps to continue. We record when recognition actually starts, whether it starts after selection or a tap to continue, whether it succeeds, fails, or is cancelled, and when results are actually shown. For native TideTone PDFs, we separately record when the preview is actually shown and when a restore completes after you confirm it. Temporary random IDs only link steps in the same flow or attempt. Recognition analytics is never used to track users and does not upload original images or PDFs, recognized text, filenames, score data, phone numbers, account identifiers, scales, time signatures, or exact quota counts. The retention terms below also apply to recognition analytics. There is currently no fixed automatic deletion period for raw records, backups, or aggregate reports.

For Seed to Song, we record when the setup screen is shown, whether access is free or Pro, the quota status shown, taps to upgrade, returns to the setup screen after a successful purchase or restore, and taps to continue. We also record when generation actually starts, whether it starts from Generate or Continue, its outcome (success, failure, or cancellation), and when the result is actually shown. Temporary random IDs only link steps within the same flow or attempt. These records omit motif notes, rhythms, styles, piece length, tempo, score names, IDs and contents, exact quota counts, raw error details, audio, files, account and device identifiers, and transaction identifiers. If you previously opted in, you need to explicitly consent again to share analytics under the updated scope. Actions taken before consent are never recorded retroactively.

For advanced score reading modes (continuous scrolling and page reading), we record when a preview is shown, which mode is selected, and whether access is free or Pro. We also record when playback of the built-in demo starts and whether it finishes or is cancelled, taps to open the upgrade screen, and returns to the reading mode selection after a successful purchase or restore. We record when you actively apply a mode by selecting it or tapping to apply it after returning, and when your score is actually shown in that mode. We count the original action as complete once you tap to upgrade, return after a successful purchase or restore, choose to apply that same mode and see your score displayed in that mode, all in the same preview flow. Temporary random IDs only link steps in the same flow or attempt. These records omit your original scores, score titles and IDs, notes, scales, handpan models, tempo, exact score positions, files, audio, video, and account, device, installation, and transaction identifiers.

Transposition analytics records when the preview appears and free or Pro access; requests to hear the original or transposed score, actual playback starts and outcomes (finished, failed or cancelled); taps to upgrade; returns after a successful purchase or restore; and attempts and outcomes for Save Copy or Save and Open (success, draft-only save, failure or cancellation). The original action is complete only after a successful library save following an upgrade and return in the same flow. Temporary random IDs only link the same flow or attempt. Records omit score names, IDs and contents, notes, scales, handpan models, transposition mappings, positions, files, audio, and account, device or transaction identifiers.

Records also include the event time, server receipt time, app version, and operating environment. Randomly generated IDs distinguish individual records or connect steps in the same flow or the same export, generation, or save attempt. For single scores and multi-score packs, the flow ID can link sharing steps with purchases or restores in that same flow.

We use these records to understand and improve TideTone’s features and purchase experience. We do not use these records for advertising tracking or sell them. The usage records uploaded by the TideTone app omit phone numbers, account, device, and installation identifiers, original transaction IDs, receipts, payment details, pack names, score names and contents, selected score IDs, share codes, clipboard contents, PDF or video files or their contents, audio, and photos.

Records are sent over HTTPS to a Tencent Cloud server used by TideTone. Raw records are stored on that server, with backups in private Tencent Cloud Object Storage (COS). There is currently no fixed automatic deletion period for raw records or backups, so they may be retained indefinitely.

You can turn off Share Usage Analytics at any time. Your device stops collecting and uploading analytics and clears unsent records. Turning sharing off does not affect purchases, restores, or Pro access. The app stops retrying unsent records once they are more than 90 days old. Turning off sharing or deleting your account does not automatically delete records already received by the server or their backups. The records have no account identifiers, so we cannot automatically find or delete records associated with a specific account. For privacy or deletion requests, email Tidetoneapp@gmail.com.

2. 我们如何使用这些信息

  • 实现云端同步：将您的谱子备份到云端，可在多设备间访问。
  • 实现分享功能：通过短码与他人分享您创作的谱子。
  • 维持登录状态：记住您的登录信息，避免重复登录。

3. 数据存储与安全

  • 谱子数据通过腾讯云对象存储（COS）加密存储，访问权限通过临时安全令牌（STS）管理。
  • 我们不会出售、出租或共享您的个人数据给第三方用于商业目的。
  • 我们不会将您的数据用于广告追踪。

4. 第三方服务

TideTone 使用以下第三方服务：

  • Sign in with Apple：Apple 的隐私保护登录服务。
  • 腾讯云：用于托管使用统计服务。腾讯云对象存储（COS）用于存储云端谱子数据和使用统计备份。
  • Apple App Store：用于处理应用内购买，Apple 的隐私政策适用。

5. 数据保留与删除

您可在 App 的设置中删除此设备上的账户信息、该账户的本地曲谱和课程进度。如需处理云端数据，请通过下方邮箱联系我们。使用统计的保留与删除见可选的使用统计。

You can use Settings in the app to remove your account information, locally stored scores, and course progress for that account from this device. To request deletion of cloud data, use the contact email below. For analytics retention and deletion, see Optional usage analytics.

6. 儿童隐私

TideTone 不针对 13 岁以下儿童。我们不会故意收集 13 岁以下儿童的个人信息。

7. 隐私政策的变更

如果我们对本隐私政策做出重大变更，我们将在 App 内通知您。继续使用 TideTone 即表示您同意更新后的隐私政策。 分享使用统计仍需您在设置中单独明确同意。
